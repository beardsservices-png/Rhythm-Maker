Play with the mouse, or the computer keyboard — Z S X D C V G B H N J M for the lower octave, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U for the one above. Arrow keys shift the range. Hold Shift to slide from the note you're on into the next one instead of restarting it.

Bassline

—

Click a cell to place a note, click it again to clear it. One note per column — a bassline is one note at a time. The S button under a column slides into that note from the one before instead of restarting it. Bars are marked every four columns.

Live looper

Press Rec and play — recording starts on the nearest bar line, so being a little early or late doesn't matter. Press Stop and it snaps to a bar and starts looping immediately. Turning a loop off and back on brings it in in time with the song, not from its own beginning. Use headphones: on speakers the microphone records the backing track into your loop. If your layers drift late, nudge Timing trim.

Export

Renders the drums, the bassline and any loop that's switched on into a single audio file you can send to anyone. It's rendered rather than recorded, so it comes out clean no matter what the browser is doing — and faster than real time.

Save your work

Saves the bassline, the tempo, your sound settings and every recorded loop — to the server, not this browser, so it's still there on your phone. Click a name to load it, shift-click to delete it.

Shape the sound

The punch is a fast pitch drop into the note, set as a ratio above it rather than fixed Hz — so a low note and a high note keep the same character. Drive is what makes a sub note audible on a phone speaker: the fundamental is below what the speaker can reproduce, so you hear the harmonics instead.
