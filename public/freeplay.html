How this works

Pick an instrument family and tone variant, then hit Randomize for a 32-step pattern spread across whole, half, quarter, and eighth notes — or click any cell to toggle a step by hand. Press Play to loop it on the Mississippi beat counter. Save a pattern to name it and keep it; shift-click a saved pattern to delete it.
