How this works

Pick an instrument and a song. Each note lights up on the strip with its fingering (flute) or key (piano). Press Start listening and play — the app listens through your microphone, and when it hears the right note held steady it flashes green, chimes, and moves on. Flute counts the note in any octave; piano wants the octave shown, unless you change that in Settings. Build your own song by tapping or typing note names, and save it to come back to. Nothing to install — it just needs microphone permission, and works best in Chrome or Firefox.

A practice aid, not the last word. Flute fingerings follow a standard chart — if your band method book shows a note differently, follow your book and your director first.
